## What changed on the TV

**Disabled (21, this run)** — Xiaomi and Google telemetry (`com.miui.tv.analytics`,
`com.xiaomi.statistic`, `com.google.android.feedback`, `com.google.android.partnersetup`), PatchWall
and its content rows (`com.mitv.tvhome.atv`, `.michannel`, `.mitvplus`, `com.xm.webcontent`), the two
auto-install configs (`android.autoinstalls.config.{xiaomi,xioami}.mibox3` and the mis-spelled
duplicate), contacts and calendar sync, the unused preloads (Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube Music, Play
Games, TalkBack, Xiaomi video player, print spooler), Ambient Mode (`com.google.android.backdrop`), and
the Xiaomi update service — that last one by choice, so firmware updates now only arrive if it is
re-enabled.

**Kept** — YouTube, Jellyfin, Play Store, Google Play Services, Google Assistant, Chromecast, the TV
Settings app, and everything Android needs to boot. `pm disable-user` was used throughout: nothing was
uninstalled.

**Replaced** — the stock launcher with `com.gathin.tvhome` v3.3 (pure Java, no network permission),
dock holding YouTube and Jellyfin with `fill_dock` off, and `live_tv_package` empty because a stick has
neither tuner nor HDMI input.

**Verified after a cold reboot** — Home still the new launcher, 39 packages still disabled, nothing
re-enabled by the firmware.

## What the session could not solve

- **Google Photos as a screensaver.** The TV is Android TV 11, where the album picker is part of the
  stock launcher's Ambient Mode settings. With the stock launcher disabled the entry point
  (`com.google.android.katniss…AmbientRedirectActivity`) starts and immediately closes. Live options:
  keep Jellyfin's screensaver (already configured; add the album to Jellyfin as a photo library), or
  install a third-party app that declares its own `DreamService`.
- **Network debugging is still on.** Port 5555, plaintext, authorised for this machine. It was left on
  in case more on-device work followed; it should be switched off and the authorisations revoked.

## Cost of doing it again

The debloat itself was quick — one script run. Nearly all the elapsed time went into environment
problems that are now documented once, in
[`docs/windows-setup-notes.md`](windows-setup-notes.md): five separate failures traced back to a single
cause (processes spawned by spawned processes cannot write outside the workspace), plus the broken
schannel TLS, the venv and pip issues, and the Android SDK and Gradle environment. A second run on the
same machine should be a fraction of this.
